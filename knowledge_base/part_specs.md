# Incoming Inspection Specifications - Northgate Cycles Ltd

All limits are inclusive. Values in other units must be converted (1 MPa = 145.038 psi; 1 inch = 25.4 mm).

## NG-HT-7005-STEM  (Alloy stem, 7005-T6, 90mm)
- tensile_strength: min 350 MPa
- yield_strength: min 290 MPa
- hardness: 85 to 100 HRB
- anodize_thickness: 10 to 25 um (REQUIRED, numeric value must be reported)
- bore_diameter: 31.75 to 31.85 mm

## NG-HT-CR-M10  (Chromoly axle bolt M10x1.25)
- tensile_strength: min 800 MPa
- yield_strength: min 640 MPa
- elongation: min 12 %
- hardness: 23 to 34 HRC
- plating_thickness: min 5 um (REQUIRED, numeric value must be reported, PASS alone is not accepted)
- thread_gauge: GO/NO-GO accept

## NG-HT-CR-M8  (Chromoly bolt M8x1.25)
- tensile_strength: min 800 MPa
- yield_strength: min 640 MPa
- hardness: 23 to 34 HRC
