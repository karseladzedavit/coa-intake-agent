# Vendor Quality Agreements - Northgate Cycles Ltd

Clauses below override the general policy for the named vendor only.

## Taiwan Precision Alloys (V-014)

TPA certificates numbered in the TPA-COA series are accepted without a customer PO on the certificate. The PO is matched from the shipment note by purchasing, so a missing PO on a TPA certificate is not a review item.

TPA anodising is done in house and the anodize thickness must always be reported as a number. No exception applies to a missing or PASS-only anodize result.

## Midwest Forge & Fastener (V-031)

Midwest Forge reports zinc plating as PASS or FAIL only. PASS is acceptable when the certificate carries a report number, because the lab retains the plating measurements for three years and provides them on request. A PASS with a report number is treated as review, not hold.

Midwest Forge reports strength in psi. When the converted value falls short of a minimum limit by less than 0.5 MPa the shortfall is a rounding artifact of the unit conversion and is treated as borderline rather than out of specification.

## Bavaria Anodising (V-052)

Bavaria holds conditional approval. Certificates that do not cite the quarterly audit reference in the format BA-AUD-YYYY-QN are placed in review even when every value is within specification.
