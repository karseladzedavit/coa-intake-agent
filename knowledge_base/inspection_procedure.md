# Incoming Inspection Procedure IP-03

## Scope

This procedure applies to all supplier certificates of analysis and certificates of conformance received for production parts. It covers document acceptance, unit handling, and the handling of results that are missing, ambiguous or close to a limit.

## Units and conversion

Suppliers may report in the units of their own test equipment. Strength values are compared in MPa; 1 MPa equals 145.038 psi. Dimensions are compared in millimetres; 1 inch equals 25.4 mm. Coating thickness is compared in micrometres; 1 mil equals 25.4 um. Hardness scales are not converted; a result reported on a scale other than the one in the specification is treated as an unknown unit and sent to review.

## Accepted test methods

Tensile and yield strength per ASTM E8 or ISO 6892. Rockwell hardness per ASTM E18. Anodic coating thickness per ISO 2360 or ASTM B244. Zinc plating per ASTM B633. Thread acceptance by GO/NO-GO gauge. Results obtained by other methods are accepted only if the method is named on the certificate.

## Reading scanned certificates

Image-only certificates are read twice by independent readers and a third time if the two readings disagree. A value that cannot be confirmed by a majority of readings is flagged with reduced confidence. Faxed or photographed certificates below 150 dpi are expected to produce reduced confidence and are routed to human review as a matter of course.

## Records

Every processed certificate is logged with its extracted values, the specification used, the policy clauses applied and the resulting decision. Logs are retained for the life of the product plus two years.
